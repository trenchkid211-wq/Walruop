About

Wal grew up in the Kakuma Refugee Camp in Kenya before starting his modeling career. He made his major runway debut for Prada in January 2023, and has since walked for Louis Vuitton, Maison Margiela, Saint Laurent and Givenchy.

Represented by Viva Model Management in Paris and Heroes Model Management in New York. Available for runway, editorial and campaign bookings.
